# Audio Grammar

- Sound is opt-in and never carries mandatory information.
- AMBIENCE, SFX and MUSIC have independent gain stages below MASTER.
- One-shot cues use scene IDs and a 1.5-second reverse-scroll cooldown.
- Outdated cues are skipped on fast scroll.
- Corridor ambience crossfades from rain; target motif appears only at SC06.
- Spatial movement is subtle: first heel right-of-centre, second closer to centre.
- Page hide suspends cues; returning resumes only ambience after user opt-in.
