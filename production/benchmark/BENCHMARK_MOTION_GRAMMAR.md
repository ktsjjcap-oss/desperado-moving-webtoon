# Motion Grammar

- Motion marks narrative punctuation: notice, stop, approach, calculate, fail, reveal.
- Default cadence is hold → one micro action → short camera settle → hold.
- Character motion outranks camera motion: pupil, blink, shoulder, breath, hand, foot.
- Parallax is restrained to 1–4%; push-ins stay under 5%.
- Action timing is time-driven after a threshold, not dragged frame-by-frame by scroll.
- Fast scrolling resolves to the outgoing keyframe.
- Reduced-motion mode preserves all story information and stable art.
