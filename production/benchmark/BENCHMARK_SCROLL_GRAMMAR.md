# Scroll Grammar

- Continuous vertical reading follows the Marvel Infinity/DC GO! model.
- Short scenes consume 0.55–0.80 viewport; ordinary scenes consume at most 1.15 viewport.
- Enter at 22–30% visibility, play a fixed-duration micro timeline, hold the stable panel, then hand off.
- Scroll velocity selects a stable key state when a user skips quickly.
- Reverse entry restores the previous stable state; time-driven action reverses once, without audio spam.
- CSS view timelines are limited to rain, progress and very cheap ambient drift.
- No repeated 180–250vh sticky scenes; total authored story target is 8.4 viewport.
