## MOTION GRAMMAR
1. SCRUBBED: y/x/scale/parallax/opacity tied to scroll and fully reversible.
2. MICRO LOOP: rain/light/breathing/glint only while visible; subtle enough that a screenshot remains a commercial webtoon frame.
3. TRIGGER: SFX or blink may fire on threshold; must not spam when user oscillates around threshold; use hysteresis/cooldown.
4. HOLD: deliberate static zones are mandatory. Constant motion = FAIL.

## BALLOONS
Balloon reveal follows reading order, not animation spectacle. Never cover face/eye/hand/tool/Silver Lily/case contact. If text does not fit, redesign composition; do not shrink below comfortable mobile reading size.

## REVERSE BEHAVIOR
Up-scroll reverses all scrubbed transforms/opacity/reveals. Triggered audio does not literally play backward; on reverse, fade/stop or reset state. Re-entering downward may retrigger only after leaving reset threshold.

## MOBILE QA
Primary viewport: 390×844 logical CSS px and 1080×1920 reference. Desktop centers mobile reader. Test slow scroll, fast flick, stop mid-beat, reverse mid-beat, repeated direction changes.

## COMMERCIAL FAIL CONDITIONS
- Looks like slideshow/PPT/Ken Burns.
- Small 16:9 cards stacked in a phone frame.
- Motion everywhere.
- Character drift or generic AI faces.
- Dark concept-art wash.
- Sound required to understand story.
- Balloons collide with focal art.
- Scroll reveal leaks information early.
- Reverse causes popping, duplicate SFX, or broken state.
- Silver Lily/case geography changes.
